Ion-mobility mass spectrometry — datasets carrying a mobility dimension (drift-tube DTIMS, trapped-ion TIMS/PASEF, traveling-wave TWIMS), converted to mzPeak with the mobility axis preserved per spectrum/peak.

<b>Provenance.</b> Openly published ion-mobility runs spanning the major IMS technologies — Agilent 6560 drift-tube (DTIMS), Bruker timsTOF trapped-ion mobility (PASEF), and additional PRIDE deposits. Each dataset names its accession and source.

## datasets

### agilent-6560-dtims-imqtof
Agilent 6560 IM-QTOF — <b>drift-tube</b> ion mobility (DTIMS) of a CE-MS standard mix; the tile's DTIMS reference, 0.3 MB mzPeak. Zenodo 18481720.

### PXD052561
Waters SELECT SERIES <b>Cyclic IMS</b> (+ Xevo G2-XS ToF) — <b>top-down</b> proteoform analysis of forest cobra (<i>Naja melanoleuca</i>) venom with ECD/CID fragmentation. 0.6 GB vendor <code>.raw</code>; cyclic ion mobility. PRIDE PXD052561.

### PXD059079
Bruker <b>timsTOF HT</b> (TIMS/PASEF) with a companion Orbitrap Ascend <b>FAIMS</b> run — <b>single-cell</b> DIA proteomics of drug-resistant prostate-cancer cells (<i>Homo sapiens</i>). 19.7 GB of deposited raw; one representative timsTOF run of 27 is published as mzPeak. PRIDE PXD059079.

### PXD076703
Bruker <b>timsTOF HT</b> (TIMS/PASEF) — DIA <b>affinity purification</b> (anti-FLAG co-IP) of FLAG-HA-tagged FAM98A/B/C from human HEK Flp-In T-REx 293 cells. 10.3 GB raw → 10.6 GB mzPeak, the largest archive in this tile. PRIDE PXD076703.

### PXD077098
Waters <b>Synapt</b> — travelling-wave ion mobility (TWIMS), DIA proteomics of the maize (<i>Zea mays</i>) seed-borne microbiome under DNA hypomethylation. 10.4 GB vendor <code>.raw</code>. PRIDE PXD077098.

### PXD078573
Bruker <b>timsTOF SCP</b> (TIMS/PASEF) — <b>chemical cross-linking MS</b> (XL-MS) resolving the U1 snRNP–RNA-polymerase-II interaction via Prp40 in <i>Saccharomyces cerevisiae</i>. 1.5 GB raw → 1.6 GB mzPeak. PRIDE PXD078573.

### PXD079072
Thermo Orbitrap Fusion Lumos with a <b>FAIMS</b> interface — field-asymmetric ion mobility, bottom-up LC-MS/MS of hemimethylated-CG-guided histone ubiquitylation in <i>Xenopus laevis</i>. 1.3 GB raw → 0.7 GB mzPeak. PRIDE PXD079072.

### PXD079300
Bruker <b>timsTOF Pro</b> (TIMS/PASEF) — DIA and DDA proteomics of boar (<i>Sus scrofa domesticus</i>) seminal-plasma <b>extracellular vesicles</b>. 2.6 GB raw → 2.7 GB mzPeak. PRIDE PXD079300.

### PXD079445
Thermo Orbitrap Exploris 480 with a <b>FAIMS</b> interface (compensation-voltage stepping) — DIA proteomics of <b>extracellular vesicles</b> from primary-tumour, recurrence and metastasis sarcoma patient-derived cultures (<i>Homo sapiens</i>). 1.4 GB raw → 0.5 GB mzPeak. PRIDE PXD079445.
